How to pick a photo that turns out well

A selfie taken in a dim room clashes with a sunlit scene. Use a photo taken somewhere bright, like near a window.

In general, a photo where your face is clear and in focus works best. If more than one person appears in the photo it's unclear whose face to use, so upload a solo shot.

FAQ

Are there swimsuit concepts?

Some concepts include resort-style swimwear. If that feels like too much, pick a dress- or shirt-based concept instead.

Can I make these in winter?

Yes. You can generate them any time, regardless of season.

How long does it take?

Usually 1–3 minutes. Generating several photos at once can take longer. You can close the app while it's working — you'll get a notification when it's done.

Will I end up looking like a different person?

The generator keeps your facial features and only changes the background, outfit, and mood. That said, it's an AI-generated image, so it won't be a 100% match — a forward-facing, in-focus photo gives the closest likeness.
