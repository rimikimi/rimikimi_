How to pick a photo that turns out well

A photo with a clear expression brings the setting to life more. A defined gaze works better than a blank expression.

In general, a photo where your face is clear and in focus works best. If more than one person appears in the photo it's unclear whose face to use, so upload a solo shot.

FAQ

Can I become an existing character?

It doesn't reproduce a specific character from an existing work — it only creates a setting with a similar mood.

Does my face change a lot?

Concepts with heavy makeup or styling can look noticeably different. If you want your face to stay recognizable, pick a concept with lighter styling.

How long does it take?

Usually 1–3 minutes. Generating several photos at once can take longer. You can close the app while it's working — you'll get a notification when it's done.

Will I end up looking like a different person?

The generator keeps your facial features and only changes the background, outfit, and mood. That said, it's an AI-generated image, so it won't be a 100% match — a forward-facing, in-focus photo gives the closest likeness.

Can I try it for free?

Yes — after you sign up you get 1 free photo a day. Want more? Top up with credits. Generated photos are automatically deleted from the server after 24 hours.
