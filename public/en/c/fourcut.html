What Does an AI Photo Booth Strip Look Like?

Upload one selfie and get a strip of four photos in a single image. Each frame gets a different pose and a printed frame, similar to a Korean photo-booth strip.

You can choose anywhere from 2 to 8 frames, and the style — school uniform, wedding, Christmas — changes the mood of the whole strip. This is modeled on Korea's 인생네컷 (in-saeng-ne-keot) — pop-up photo booths that print a strip of photos in a few minutes — except here you upload one photo instead of stepping into a booth, and each frame gets a different pose automatically. What you don't get is the fun of the moment itself with a friend in a real booth — for that, the actual booth still wins.

How to pick a photo that turns out well

More frames means each one is smaller. Four frames or fewer keeps faces clear.

In general, a photo where your face is clear and in focus works best. If more than one person appears in the photo it's unclear whose face to use, so upload a solo shot.

FAQ

How many frames can I get?

You can choose anywhere from 2 to 8 frames. Credits used scale with the number of frames.

Can I get a couple's photo-booth strip too?

Yes. Choosing a couple style makes a strip with both people together. That's separate from the Couple concept, which takes two uploaded photos.

How long does it take?

Usually 1–3 minutes. Generating several photos at once can take longer. You can close the app while it's working — you'll get a notification when it's done.

Will I end up looking like a different person?

The generator keeps your facial features and only changes the background, outfit, and mood. That said, it's an AI-generated image, so it won't be a 100% match — a forward-facing, in-focus photo gives the closest likeness.
