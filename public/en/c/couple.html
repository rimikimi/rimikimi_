How Many Photos Do You Need for AI Couple Photos?

Two. Upload one selfie from each person and get a photo of both of you together in the same scene. You don't need a photo you actually took together.

Useful when you're long-distance and don't have a photo together, or when the photos you do have together didn't turn out well. Since each face is generated from its own photo and placed into one scene, you can get a couple photo even from pictures taken on different days in different places. The one thing that matters is that both photos are similar in quality — if one is dark or blurry, that person will look off next to the other.

How to pick a photo that turns out well

Use photos where both people are facing forward. If one is in profile, the composition looks unnatural.

In general, a photo where your face is clear and in focus works best. If more than one person appears in the photo it's unclear whose face to use, so upload a solo shot.

FAQ

Does it have to be a couple?

No — it works for any two people. Friends, family, and siblings turn out the same way.

How do I upload two photos?

Choosing a Couple concept gives you two upload slots. Add one photo to each.

How long does it take?

Usually 1–3 minutes. Generating several photos at once can take longer. You can close the app while it's working — you'll get a notification when it's done.

Will I end up looking like a different person?

The generator keeps your facial features and only changes the background, outfit, and mood. That said, it's an AI-generated image, so it won't be a 100% match — a forward-facing, in-focus photo gives the closest likeness.
