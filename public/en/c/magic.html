How Is the Magic Booth Different From Regular Concepts?

These aren't about changing your face — they're tools that work on the photo itself, like restoring an old photo or trying on clothes.

Where a regular concept makes "you in a different scene," the Magic Booth is closer to "fixing this exact photo." That's why some of these tools accept photos without a face, and the output often keeps the original photo's aspect ratio. What photo you need depends on the tool, so check each concept's instructions before uploading.

How to pick a photo that turns out well

Restoration tools can work with a blurry original, but a photo where the face isn't visible at all is hard to recover.

In general, a photo where your face is clear and in focus works best. If more than one person appears in the photo it's unclear whose face to use, so upload a solo shot.

FAQ

How does the Dressing Room work?

Upload one photo of yourself along with a photo of the garment, and you'll get a photo of yourself wearing it. You can upload up to 5 garment photos.

How much can photo restoration bring back?

Blur and damage improve significantly, but since it's filling in information that wasn't in the original, the result may not exactly match reality.

How long does it take?

Usually 1–3 minutes. Generating several photos at once can take longer. You can close the app while it's working — you'll get a notification when it's done.

Will I end up looking like a different person?

The generator keeps your facial features and only changes the background, outfit, and mood. That said, it's an AI-generated image, so it won't be a 100% match — a forward-facing, in-focus photo gives the closest likeness.
