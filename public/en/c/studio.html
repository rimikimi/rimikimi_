How to pick a photo that turns out well

A front-facing photo with little shadow on the face works best. Hats, sunglasses, and heavy filters hide facial features and make results less consistent.

In general, a photo where your face is clear and in focus works best. If more than one person appears in the photo it's unclear whose face to use, so upload a solo shot.

FAQ

Can I use this as an ID photo?

We don't recommend it for official submissions. The proportions — background color, face ratio, shadow rules — may not match official specs, and passport or visa reviewers can reject it. There's a separate ID Photo concept, but treat that as a reference too, not an official document photo.

Can I change the outfit?

Each concept has its own fixed outfit — suit, shirt, knit. Pick the concept whose outfit matches what you want. If you want to wear clothes you actually own, use the Dressing Room feature instead.

How long does it take?

Usually 1–3 minutes. Generating several photos at once can take longer. You can close the app while it's working — you'll get a notification when it's done.

Will I end up looking like a different person?

The generator keeps your facial features and only changes the background, outfit, and mood. That said, it's an AI-generated image, so it won't be a 100% match — a forward-facing, in-focus photo gives the closest likeness.
